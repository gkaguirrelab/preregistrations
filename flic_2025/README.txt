Pre-registration files related to our study of flicker discrimination using a split-field dichoptic rig.

An initial experiment ("Summer2025") was described and started. During study of two initial participants we identified several deficiencies in the experimental design. Ultimately, we determined that the task was too difficulty for naive observers to perform. Therefore this experiment was abandoned on August 13, 2025. The data collected from these two observers will not be reported or used in a publication.

We designed an alternate approach to measuring discrimination thresholds (using a "same / different" paradigm that is susceptible to the observer response criterion). The pre-registration document ("Fall2025") was uploaded on August 18, 2025 and the experiment was restarted.
